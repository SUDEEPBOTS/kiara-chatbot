## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within Kiara Chatbot, please do **NOT** open a public issue.

Instead, please send an advisory or email to the repository maintainer privately. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and provide a fix or mitigation timeline promptly.
